## Website Development Cost in Bangalore (2026)

Pricing varies depending on complexity, features, and business needs.

### Typical cost breakdown:

- **Basic website (5–7 pages):** ₹10,000 – ₹25,000  
- **Business website:** ₹25,000 – ₹60,000  
- **E-commerce website:** ₹30,000 – ₹1,00,000+  
- **Custom web applications:** ₹50,000+

### Factors affecting cost:

- Design complexity
- Number of pages
- Features (payment, booking, etc.)
- SEO & performance optimization
